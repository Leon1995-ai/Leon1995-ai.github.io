ACADEMIC PROFILE

About

I am a Lecturer in the School of Computer Science at Qufu Normal University. I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Dalian Maritime University in 2025, supervised by Prof. Zhenyu Na and Prof. Bin Lin.

My work investigates the joint optimization of sensing, communication, computing, and caching resources in dynamic wireless networks. Current interests include multi-timescale service orchestration and digital-twin-assisted decision-making under network and resource uncertainty.

RESEARCH

Research Interests

01NETWORK SYSTEMS

Integrated Space–Air–Ground–Sea Networks

Coordinated networking and resource allocation across satellites, HAPs, UAVs, and terrestrial and maritime nodes, with an emphasis on dynamic connectivity and resource constraints.

02RESOURCE OPTIMIZATION

Edge Computing & Resource Orchestration

Joint sensing–communication–computing–caching (ISCC) optimization, including task offloading, service caching, computing resource allocation, and energy-aware scheduling.

03ADAPTIVE ORCHESTRATION

Digital Twin & Intelligent Network Orchestration

Digital-twin-assisted service deployment and inference orchestration across multiple timescales, incorporating prediction and uncertainty information into network decisions.
