Profile

About Me

I am a Lecturer with the School of Computer Science, Qufu Normal University. My research focuses on integrated space–air–ground–sea networks, edge intelligence and mobile edge computing, with particular interest in resource-constrained maritime and remote environments.

My recent work studies joint communication, computing and energy management, renewable-powered edge systems, UAV/HAP-assisted networking, 6G integrated sensing–communication–computing–caching, and digital-twin-assisted AI service orchestration.
