01

About

I am a Lecturer in the School of Computer Science at Qufu Normal University. My research focuses on edge intelligence and resource orchestration for heterogeneous wireless networks, particularly UAV/HAP-assisted and integrated networks.

My current interests include predictive multi-timescale network-service co-design, uncertainty-aware resource orchestration, digital-twin-assisted edge intelligence, and reliable computing in infrastructure-limited or energy-constrained environments.
